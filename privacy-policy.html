Privacy Policy

How SHUBH AI handles contact messages and browser-local tools.

Contact messages

When the contact form is enabled, Formspree processes the name, email address, subject, and message you submit and forwards a notification to Shubham Pandey. Shubham uses these details to read and respond to your enquiry. Direct email is handled by your email provider and Gmail.

Browser tools and hosting

The text analyzer processes input in your browser. The website code does not send that input to a server or add analytics or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process request information such as IP addresses when serving pages.

Local notes and learning chat

Reading notes stay in this browser’s local storage until you clear the site data. Chat messages stay in the open page. The chat sends the selected site topic to its backend. When Groq is configured, the backend sends predefined public website facts to Groq to phrase a reply.

Questions

Email shubhamgkpp@gmail.com with questions about your contact message or to request its deletion.
